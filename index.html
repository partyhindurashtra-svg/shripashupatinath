About the Dham

Shri Pashupatinath Dham stands at Hassangarh, Sampla (Rohtak), Haryana, dedicated to Bhagwan Shiva as Pashupati — the Lord of all beings.

Its sacred Shivling was brought from the famed Pashupatinath Mandir of Kathmandu, Nepal. Built through the seva of devotees, the dham is guided today by the Param Sewak Board.

The Meaning of Pashupati

Significance

Pashupati means “Lord of all creatures” — the compassionate form of Shiva who protects every soul.

🐾 Protector of All

Shiva as the guardian who guides every soul toward liberation.

🕉️ Path to Moksha

Devotion to Pashupatinath is believed to free the soul from birth and death.

🌊 Holiest Shaiva Shrine

Pashupatinath, Kathmandu — worshipped for over 2,000 years.

🔥 Maha Shivaratri

Devotees gather to offer bael leaves, milk and prayers to Mahadev.

An Eternal Bond

The Kathmandu Connection

Our dham draws its inspiration from the legendary Pashupatinath Mandir of Kathmandu, Nepal — Nepal's holiest Shiva temple, on the banks of the sacred Bagmati.

The sacred Shivling at our dham was brought from Pashupatinath, Kathmandu, carrying its divine blessings to Hassangarh.

  • UNESCO World Heritage Site (1979)
  • Worshipped for over 2,000 years
  • Iconic gold-roofed pagoda shrine
॥ अन्नदानं महादानम् ॥

Annual Bhandara

Every year the dham hosts a grand Bhandara — a free community feast open to all.

30 November

Every year on 30th November, prasad and a wholesome meal are served to every devotee and visitor. Sabka swagat hai — all are welcome.
