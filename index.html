About the Dham

Shri Pashupatinath Dham is a sacred Shiva temple that stands in the village of Hassangarh, Sampla, District Rohtak, Haryana. Dedicated to Bhagwan Shiva in His most benevolent form as Pashupati — the Lord and protector of all living beings — the dham is a centre of devotion, seva, meditation and community upliftment.

Built stone by stone through the shraddha and seva of devotees, the temple's spiritual heart is a sacred Shivling brought from the world-renowned Pashupatinath Mandir of Kathmandu, Nepal — one of the holiest Shaiva shrines on earth — establishing an eternal bond between the two dhams.

The dham was raised from open land beside a sacred sarovar (pond). To relive that journey of faith, visit “The Making of the Dham”. Today, guided by the Param Sewak Board, it welcomes every devotee to offer prayers and share in its living tradition of faith and service.

The Meaning of Pashupati

Significance of Lord Pashupatinath

The name Pashupati (पशुपति) means "Lord of all creatures" — Pashu (all living beings) and Pati (lord, protector). It is one of the most compassionate forms of Bhagwan Shiva, who watches over every soul and liberates it from the bondage of worldly existence.

🐾 Protector of All Beings

As Pashupati, Shiva is the shepherd who guides every jiva (soul) from ignorance to liberation. He is worshipped as the compassionate guardian of all creation.

🕉️ Path to Moksha

Devotion to Pashupatinath is believed to free the soul from the endless cycle of birth and death, granting moksha — the ultimate spiritual liberation.

🌊 The Holiest Shaiva Shrine

Pashupatinath in Kathmandu, on the banks of the sacred Bagmati, is revered as the holiest temple of Lord Shiva — a religious foundation dating back over 2,000 years.

🔥 Maha Shivaratri

The dham is a living centre of Shiva worship. On Maha Shivaratri, devotees gather to offer bael leaves, milk and prayers to Mahadev.

An Eternal Bond

The Kathmandu Connection

The spiritual inspiration for Shri Pashupatinath Dham flows directly from the legendary Pashupatinath Mandir of Kathmandu, Nepal — the holiest Hindu temple of Nepal, standing on the banks of the sacred Bagmati River.

The sacred Shivling installed at our dham has been brought from Pashupatinath, Kathmandu, carrying with it the divine energy and blessings of that ancient shrine to the soil of Hassangarh.

  • UNESCO World Heritage Site — designated in 1979 for its cultural and religious importance.
  • Over 2,000 years of worship — a religious foundation dating to at least the 5th century BCE.
  • Pagoda architecture — a two-storey gilt-roofed pagoda with silver doors, surrounded by more than 500 shrines.
  • Sacred legend — it is believed Shiva and Parvati once roamed the Kathmandu valley in the form of deer, marking this holy ground.
"Dying and being cremated at Pashupatinath is believed to ensure moksha — liberation from the cycle of birth and rebirth."

— On the sanctity of Pashupatinath, Kathmandu

Annual Bhandara — Seva of Annadान

In the spirit of Mahadev's boundless compassion, Shri Pashupatinath Dham hosts a grand annual Bhandara — a free community feast open to all, without distinction.

30 November

Every Year on 30th November
Prasad and a wholesome meal are served to every devotee and visitor who comes to the dham. Annadान — the giving of food — is among the highest forms of daan, and all are warmly invited to partake and to serve.

🙏 All devotees and well-wishers are warmly invited to attend and to serve. Sabka swagat hai — everyone is welcome.
